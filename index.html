      Privacy & Developer

      Masks your account name wherever it's shown in the launcher (header, account switcher) — the real name is still used to launch the game.

      Signs every account out when the launcher fully closes. Accounts stay saved — you'll just need to pick one again next launch.

      Replaces your home folder and game directory paths in logs with generic placeholders instead of showing them in full.

      Adds verbose DEBUG-level entries (like the exact launch command, subject to the settings above) to the log and console windows.

      Experimental

      Shows a "likely cause" popup after a crash, based on pattern-matching the log — not a full rules engine, so it can occasionally misread the real cause or suggest a fix that doesn't actually help. Turn this off if it's giving you bad guesses; you can still read the raw log yourself either way.
